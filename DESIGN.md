# VeriHire AI Design System

## Branding & Identity
**Product Name:** VeriHire AI
**Tagline:** Real-Time Recruitment Threat Intelligence Platform
**Aesthetic:** Futuristic, cybersecurity-oriented, enterprise-grade, premium AI dashboard.

## Color Palette
### Core Colors
- **Background:** `#000000` (True Black)
- **Surface:** `#09090b` (Zinc 950)
- **Primary:** `#6366f1` (Indigo 500)
- **Secondary:** `#a855f7` (Purple 500)
- **Accent:** `#3f3f46` (Zinc 700 / Glassmorphism borders)

### Functional Colors
- **Critical Risk:** `#ef4444` (Red 500)
- **Warning/High Risk:** `#f97316` (Orange 500)
- **Safe/Low Risk:** `#22c55e` (Green 500)
- **Info:** `#3b82f6` (Blue 500)

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** `Inter`, `system-ui`, sans-serif
- **Heading Font:** `Satoshi` or `Geist Sans`
- **Mono Font:** `Geist Mono` or `JetBrains Mono` (for data/code elements)

## UI Elements
### Glassmorphism
- **Background Blur:** `12px`
- **Border Opacity:** `0.1` (White or Primary)
- **Background Opacity:** `0.05` to `0.1`

### Effects
- **Glow:** Subtle outer glows for primary buttons and high-risk alerts.
- **Borders:** Ultra-thin `1px` borders with subtle gradients.
- **Gradients:** `linear-gradient(to bottom right, #6366f1, #a855f7)` for primary actions.

## Tone & Voice
- **Professional:** Direct and authoritative.
- **Secure:** Emphasizing safety and intelligence.
- **Modern:** Using tech-forward language.

## Design Components Inspiration
- **Linear:** Minimalistic, high-density information, beautiful typography.
- **Vercel:** Clean, fast, developer-centric.
- **Stripe:** Smooth transitions, premium feel.
